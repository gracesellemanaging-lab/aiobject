Privacy

Local by design

  • Camera frames are analysed on this computer and are never uploaded to a cloud AI service.
  • History thumbnails are stored under data/snapshots; metadata lives in data/history.db.
  • Reference images come from Wikimedia and are cached locally as public educational content.
  • The app does not identify people by name, face or personal identity.
Honest limits

What is not supported

Mango, grapes, watermelon, papaya and dragon fruit are not in the supported fruit vocabulary and will not be guessed. Objects outside the COCO training set are also not claimed as recognised.
